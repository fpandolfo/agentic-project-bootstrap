# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive and technically rigorous community.

Be constructive, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people, protect
private information, and make space for contributors with different levels of
experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and deliberate abuse
of project infrastructure are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact options on the
GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and proportionally.
