2. Identify the exact decision to verify.
3. Prefer official docs/specifications/release notes.
4. Separate observed current fact, inference and project recommendation.
5. Record material durable decisions with source, date checked, decision, validation and revisit trigger.
6. Never place secrets in docs, prompts or research artifacts.
